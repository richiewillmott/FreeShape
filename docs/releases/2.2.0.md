# FreeShape 2.2.0

Whole features from Onshape's Part Studio menu, variables measured from the model, smarter selection, and
Onshape's view options.

## Highlights

**New features** — **Thicken**, **Enclose**, **Rib**, **Helix**, **Delete part** and **Bounding box**, and from
SALOME, **Compound**: solids as one part that share the faces where they touch (glue faces) — for conformal
meshing; *Shape info…* shows the topology counts, and BREP export keeps the shared faces. **Explode** splits a
compound back into its solids, or any part into faces or edges.
[FEATURE_PARITY.md](https://github.com/richiewillmott/FreeShape/blob/master/FEATURE_PARITY.md) now covers
**93%** of the listed Onshape options.

**Measure value: variables measured from the model**
- Select two vertices (of parts, surfaces or sketches) or edges and the corner bar shows `Min dist: … mm`.
  Press **(x)** and it becomes a `#variable` that is re-measured on every rebuild — so dimensions and features
  that use it follow the model. A face or surface body gives its **area**, a solid its **volume**, an edge or
  sketch curve its **length** (a round one its **diameter**).
- The feature has the options of the *Measure value* FeatureScript for Onshape: minimum, maximum, centre or
  infinite-propagation distance; ray distance; angle (with flip); perimeter; area; volume; count of matching faces
  or edges; coordinates (global, local or along a direction); centroid; bounding box; inertia; rounding. A plane
  or an axis stands in for a mate connector.
- Vertices are selectable by default, and sketch points (line ends, centres) can be picked in the 3D view.

**Smarter selection** — edges or sketch lines picked before Extrude, Revolve or Sweep make a **surface** from
exactly those edges. Switching **Solid → Thin / Surface** keeps the selection, turning faces into their outlines.
Solid / Surface / Thin are buttons, and surfaces can **Add** into the surfaces they meet.
**Non-conformal surfaces** — curves that branch or cross (a +, a T, two crossing lines) make one surface per arm
in a single feature — a + gives four — where Onshape fails; *Thin* makes them one solid wall.

**View options, as in Onshape** — under the view cube and in **View ▸ Display**:
- Isometric, **dimetric** and **trimetric** views; **previous view** (Alt+Left); **zoom to window** (Shift+Z);
  **named views**, saved in the file; **perspective** view.
- Shaded with or without edges, **unshaded** or **translucent**; hidden edges removed or dashed; tangent edges
  shown, hidden or as phantom lines; **high quality**; **highlighted boundary edges** of surfaces.
- **Graphics preferences…** gathers them in one window; your choices are remembered.

**Fixed** — View ▸ Front … Isometric and Shift+1 … Shift+7 turn the view again.

Full details: [CHANGES.md](https://github.com/richiewillmott/FreeShape/blob/master/CHANGES.md).

## Download

- **Windows:** `FreeShape-Setup-2.2.0.exe` — installs over 2.1. SmartScreen may warn because the installer
  isn't code-signed yet: **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `FreeShape-2.2.0-mac-arm64.dmg` — drag into Applications; the first time,
  right-click → **Open** → **Open**.
- **Linux:** `FreeShape-2.2.0-x86_64.AppImage` — `chmod +x` it and run it.

## Upgrading from 2.1

- Everything made in 2.1 opens and rebuilds the same. Surfaces made in 2.1 stay *New*.
- Clicking near a corner now selects the **vertex** (as in Onshape). To go back to the old behaviour, untick
  **Select ▸ Vertices**.
- A file that uses the new features (Thicken, Measure value …) won't rebuild in 2.1 — keep a copy if you
  might need to go back.

## Not yet

Conic fillets, loft guide curves and start / end tangency, parting-line draft, rib draft and "extend profiles up
to part" are still to do; sweep takes one guide curve. See FEATURE_PARITY.md.
