# FreeShape 2.3.1

A fix release: fillets after threads, two Extrude dialog fixes, and clearer undo / redo icons. Files open and
rebuild the same as in 2.3.0.

## Fixes

**Fillets and chamfers after a thread** — on some models (a modelled thread, for one), every later fillet or
chamfer on that part failed as "too large", however small. The solid is now repaired first, so they work. When no
size really works, the message says so instead of "too large".

**Extrude picks New or Add for you** — a new Extrude, Revolve or Sweep now chooses **New** when it touches no part,
and **Add** when it does, with the touching part filled in as the merge scope, as Onshape does. It follows your
selection and depth until you choose an option or a merge scope yourself. Before, it always chose Add once the
model had a part, so a separate shape didn't get its own part.

**Surface and Thin from a selected area** — switching from Solid to Surface or Thin now lists the area's **edges
one by one** instead of one "Outline of sketch region", so you can remove some: take one edge off a square and you
get a C-shaped surface or wall. Switching back to Solid gives the area again if all its edges are still there.

**No more hidden solid** — with Surface or Thin chosen and nothing selected yet, clicking a face by mistake made a
solid while the dialog still showed Surface with an empty list. Surface and Thin now only take edges and sketch
curves, and the automatic choice of type only happens for things you selected before starting the feature.

## Also

Clearer **undo / redo** icons: hooked arrows, as in most apps.

## Download

- **Windows:** `FreeShape-Setup-2.3.1.exe` — installs over 2.3.0 (or the portable zip). SmartScreen may warn
  because the installer isn't code-signed yet: **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `FreeShape-2.3.1-mac-arm64.dmg` — drag into Applications; the first time,
  right-click → **Open** → **Open**.
- **Linux:** `FreeShape-2.3.1-x86_64.AppImage` — `chmod +x` it and run it.

## Upgrading from 2.3.0

Install over it. Nothing in your files changes. Existing Surface and Thin features made from a whole outline keep
working as they are.
